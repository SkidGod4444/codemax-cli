# codemax trademark policy

The codemax code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **codemax name, the halftone wordmark and the "cm" icon** are not covered by that license. They identify the official project, and this policy says how you can use them.

## You can, without asking

- Say your project is **based on codemax** or **a fork of codemax**, or that it **works with codemax**.
- Use the name to talk about codemax accurately: in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and reviews.
- Keep the name in the code, file names and git history of an unmodified copy.
- Link to the official project with the name: https://github.com/SkidGod4444/codemax-cli or https://codemax.saidev.codes.

## You can't, without written permission

- Name a fork, product, package, command, domain or company **codemax**, or something confusingly close (for example "codemax pro", "codemaxx", "code-max").
- Use the codemax wordmark or icon for your own project, or a logo that looks like them.
- Suggest that your project is official, endorsed by or affiliated with codemax or Saidev Dhal.
- Publish a modified build under the codemax name on a package registry, app store or download page.

## If you fork

Give your fork its own name and logo before you publish it. Keep the copyright and license notices in `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`, as the AGPL requires. A line such as "based on codemax" is welcome.

## Questions

Ask before you're unsure: open an issue on the repository, or contact Saidev Dhal at https://saidev.codes.

This policy may be updated as the project grows.
